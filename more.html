SKILLS

UI Design

I make UI layouts, pubmats, and slides using Canva and other tools. I usually do this for org work and school projects, so I try to keep things clear and easy to read. I like making designs that don’t feel messy and are simple to understand.

Cybersecurity

I’m interested in cybersecurity, especially how attacks are spotted and stopped. I’ve been learning about how systems get monitored and protected in class and org settings. I also like learning how hackers think so I can understand defenses better.

IT Management

I studied IT System Operations (ITSYSOP) and IT Planning (ITPLANN) in school. I also learned Lean Six Sigma basics for improving processes and making work more efficient. These helped me understand how IT systems are planned and handled in real setups.

HOBBIES

Gaming

I play games like League of Legends, Minecraft, and Skyrim when I have free time. It helps me relax and sometimes even think more creatively. It also got me interested in making maps and game-related stuff.

Art

I like doing digital art, sketches, and making pubmats for different activities. It’s one of my main ways of expressing ideas visually. I usually mix it with my interest in layouts and design.

Cooking

I cook when I want to unwind and just take a break from school stuff. I like trying Filipino dishes and sometimes Korean-inspired meals too. It’s a chill hobby that helps me slow down a bit.

ACTIVITIES

Organizations

I’m part of orgs like ISO, AWS Archers Cloud Club, CATCH2T27, and Viridis Arcus Esports. Most of the time I help out in events, planning, and other team tasks. It’s where I get to meet people and learn how teams actually work.

Freelancing

I worked as a Minecraft map creator on Fiverr for different clients. It was a mix of creative work and following client requests. It also taught me how to manage time and deliver work on schedule.

Clubs

I join reading clubs and other small groups when I have time. It’s more of a chill space for me outside school work. I like it because it helps me relax and meet people with similar interests.
