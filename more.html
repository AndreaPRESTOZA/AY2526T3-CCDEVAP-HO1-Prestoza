SKILLS

UI Design

I design UI layouts, pubmats, and presentation materials using Canva and other digital tools. This helps me communicate ideas clearly through visual design, especially for organizations and academic projects. I focus on making outputs clean, structured, and easy to understand.


Cybersecurity

I am interested in cybersecurity, particularly threat intelligence and defensive security concepts. Through academic exposure and organizational work, I continue learning how systems are protected and monitored. I also study how attackers operate and how defenses are built against them.


IT Management

I studied IT System Operations (ITSYSOP) and IT Planning (ITPLANN), which introduced structured IT management concepts. I also learned Lean Six Sigma principles for process improvement, efficiency, and workflow optimization. These helped me understand how IT systems are planned, managed, and improved in real environments.

HOBBIES

Gaming

I enjoy playing games such as League of Legends, Minecraft, and Skyrim. Gaming helps me relax while also improving creativity, strategy, and problem-solving skills. It also influenced my interest in map creation and game design.


Art

I enjoy digital art, sketching, and creating pubmats for organizations and personal projects. This hobby allows me to express creativity while practicing consistency in design and layout. I often combine it with my interest in UI and visual communication.


Cooking

Cooking is one of my relaxing hobbies where I get to explore different flavors and techniques. I enjoy experimenting with Filipino dishes as well as Korean-inspired meals. It helps me develop patience, creativity, and attention to detail.
